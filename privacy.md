# 涨脑子了么 · Sink or Read

## 隐私政策（中文）

**涨脑子了么**（以下称「本 App」）由个人开发者开发。生效日期：2026 年 11 月 10 日

### 一句话

本 App 不收集、不上传、不出售你的任何数据。没有账号，没有服务器，没有广告，没有统计分析，没有追踪。

### 本 App 在你手机上保存的内容

以下内容只保存在你的 iPhone 上（本 App 和它的小组件、屏幕使用时间扩展共用的存储空间），开发者看不到：

- 每天的打卡记录、连续天数、阅读时长；
- 你的设置：约定阅读时间、严格模式、外观、分天方式等；
- 你在「屏幕使用时间」里选择要锁的 App 和阅读 App。iOS 只给本 App 一个加密的标识，本 App 看不到这些 App 的名字；
- 微信读书 API Key（如果你填了），保存在系统钥匙串里。

删除本 App，以上内容就会一起删除。

### 屏幕使用时间

本 App 使用 Apple 的屏幕使用时间接口（Family Controls、Managed Settings、Device Activity），用来在你读满约定时间前拦截你选择的 App，以及估算你在阅读 App 里用了多久。这些数据由 iOS 在手机上处理，不会离开你的手机。你随时可以在本 App 或「设置 → 屏幕使用时间」里关闭。

### 位置

如果你允许，本 App 会获取**大致位置**，只用来计算当地日出日落时间、自动切换白天和夜晚的配色。位置只在手机上使用，不保存精确位置，也不上传。不允许也能正常使用。

### 微信读书 API Key（可选）

你可以选填自己的微信读书 API Key，用来读取你在微信读书里的阅读时长，让统计更准确。

- Key 只保存在你手机的钥匙串里，不会发给开发者或任何第三方。
- 本 App 只用它直接向微信读书官方接口（`https://i.weread.qq.com`）查询**你自己每天的阅读时长**，不读取书单、笔记或其他内容。
- 这个请求是从你的手机直接发给微信读书的，受微信读书 / 腾讯的隐私政策约束。
- 你随时可以在「设置 → 微信读书记录」里删除 Key。

### 儿童

本 App 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不收集任何人的个人信息。

### 变更

如果本政策有变化，会更新本页面并修改生效日期。

## Privacy Policy (English)

**Sink or Read** ("the App") is made by an independent developer. Effective date: November 10, 2026

### In short

The App does not collect, upload or sell any of your data. There is no account, no server, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

### What the App stores on your iPhone

The following is stored only on your iPhone (in storage shared by the App, its widget and its Screen Time extensions). The developer cannot see it:

- Your daily check-ins, streaks and reading time;
- Your settings, such as the daily reading goal, strict mode, appearance and when a day starts;
- The apps you chose to lock and your reading app, selected through Screen Time. iOS only gives the App opaque tokens; the App cannot see the names of these apps;
- Your WeChat Read (微信读书) API key, if you entered one, stored in the iOS Keychain.

Deleting the App deletes all of the above.

### Screen Time

The App uses Apple's Screen Time APIs (Family Controls, Managed Settings, Device Activity) to block the apps you chose until you have read for your daily goal, and to estimate how long you spent in your reading app. This data is processed by iOS on your device and never leaves it. You can turn this off at any time in the App or in Settings → Screen Time.

### Location

If you allow it, the App uses your **approximate location** only to calculate local sunrise and sunset and switch between day and night colors. It is used on the device only; precise location is not stored or uploaded. The App works fine without it.

### WeChat Read API key (optional)

You may enter your own WeChat Read API key so the App can read your reading time from WeChat Read for more accurate tracking.

- The key is stored only in your device's Keychain and is never sent to the developer or any other third party.
- The App uses it only to ask WeChat Read's official API (`https://i.weread.qq.com`) for **your own daily reading time**. It does not read your books, notes or anything else.
- The request goes directly from your device to WeChat Read and is subject to WeChat Read / Tencent's privacy policy.
- You can delete the key at any time in Settings → WeChat Read.

### Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not collect personal information from anyone.

If this policy changes, this page will be updated along with the effective date.
